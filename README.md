# pepe

brain dump 

My project is basically an organization list. A simple idea that came to me while I was jotting down potential projects. I looked at my list, thought it looked terrible, and decided to build this app from scratch. Even though I knew absolutely nothing about JavaScript, I felt confident because I didn't think it would be that complex. At first, I didn't have much of a plan, so I just created the title and a button to add tasks to the list. I didn't even have a way to delete them. When I created a button to mark tasks as complete, it was just a red square that didn't work. I thought the problem was in the JS, but it turned out to be a typo in the HTML. Later, I added a delete button and renamed the project "Dragon's Lair" (which was easy, as I just followed the same steps I used for the square). Then I added color and changed the typography. The visual aspect was the hardest part. I'm disappointed with how it turned out, but I'm letting it slide. It was difficult mainly because I have zero creativity, the few features I did include came from ideas suggested by friends. Since I wasn't satisfied with the look, I looked for tutorials on styling the list. I found one that gave me ideas for the animations and the list's shape. I switched to the final color scheme, inspired by the Evangelion palette, and that's roughly where I posted my first devlog. Once finished, I had plenty of ideas for continuing the project, such as adding a "streak" feature and a calendar (inspired by Stardew Valley and Duolingo). However, things went downhill from there. I got stuck in many places, which shows in my commit history. I uploaded very little progress or only minor updates. This was because I had many ideas but didn't know how to execute them properly, so I mostly focused on visual tweaks and bug fixes. I wanted to include more complex animations, but again, I didn't know how, and the tutorials didn't quite convince me. I really struggled, it shows in the code, but I learned everything through tutorials and help from a friend who has been programming for six years. By the time I finally figured out exactly what I wanted, I was out of time, so I just implemented a rough version of the visual design and wrapped it up.


This project was born out of my own lack of organization, which is exactly the issue I wanted to address. There are people like me who struggle with motivation and staying organized, which is why I included the list and the streak counter so you can track your work and progress. It's missing a few things, like animations for unlocking a streak and other visual flourishes, but I've decided to leave the project as it is for now. If I were to make a sequel, I might continue and improve upon it in the future.
